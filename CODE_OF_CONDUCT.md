# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors, maintainers, and users of NeuroMirror, regardless of experience, background, identity, or perspective.

## Expected Standards

- **Respect and Kindness**: Treat fellow collaborators with empathy and professionalism.
- **Constructive Communication**: Focus on constructive feedback and community collaboration.
- **Scientific Integrity**: Foster open, respectful discussions around anatomical modeling, computer vision, and graphics engineering.
- **Safety**: Refrain from any derogatory, abusive, discriminatory, or disruptive behavior.
